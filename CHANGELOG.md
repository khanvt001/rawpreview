# Changelog

## 2.1 (2026-11-05)

RawPreview is now open source under the MIT License.

- Renamed the 21 built-in presets named after photographers. They now describe the look, and the settings are unchanged.
- The support window now offers Ko-fi only.
- Builds from source have no analytics and need no Apple Developer account.

## 2.0 (2026-11-02)

- Presets and edits can now develop from the original RAW.
- Arrow keys stay on the photos after you use the Presets panel.

## 1.0 (2026-11-01)

First release.
